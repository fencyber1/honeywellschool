# Honeywell School Website Plan

## Goal
Create a fast, mobile-first school website that reassures parents immediately and consistently guides them toward contacting admissions.

## Visual direction
- Build the selected **Diagonal Glass Editorial** direction faithfully: Archivo typography, Fraunces accents, deep navy, trust blue, growth green, and a focused orange action color.
- Preserve its angular image framing, editorial hierarchy, restrained glass effects, credibility strip, dark campus band, and mobile contact bar.
- Generate cohesive documentary-style school imagery for students, classrooms, facilities, staff, and parent stories.
- Use restrained motion and honor reduced-motion settings.

## Pages
- **Home:** enrollment-led opening, trust signals, programs overview, campus preview, testimonial, events, and admissions enquiry.
- **About:** emotional mission, values, story, leadership, and community.
- **Programs:** scannable learning stages, curriculum highlights, enrichment, and outcomes.
- **Admissions:** requirements, fees overview, step-by-step process, deadlines, FAQs, and a display-only enquiry/application form.
- **Campus:** visual tour of classrooms, science and technology spaces, arts, sports, playgrounds, and safety features.
- **Team:** principal, teachers, and staff profiles with generated portraits and short biographies.
- **Stories:** parent testimonials, student achievements, and graduate success stories.
- **News & Events:** upcoming events, school news, achievements, and newsletter signup prototype.
- **Contact:** phone, email, WhatsApp, opening hours, address, directions/map treatment, and enquiry form.

## Conversion experience
- Make **Contact Admissions** the strongest action throughout the site.
- Add secondary phone, email, WhatsApp, tour, and application options where relevant.
- Include a persistent mobile contact bar and a compact desktop admissions shortcut.
- Add a display-only admissions chat panel with common parent questions and scripted answers.
- Use specific demonstration proof, deadlines, testimonials, fees, and achievements while visibly noting that school details must be replaced with verified information before launch.

## Navigation and usability
- Provide clear desktop navigation and a compact mobile menu.
- Keep every key parent task reachable within two interactions.
- Use accessible labels, keyboard-friendly controls, visible focus states, readable contrast, and stable layouts across phone and desktop sizes.
- Ensure every linked section has a working page rather than a dead link.

## Technical details
- Build all pages with the existing TanStack routing structure and shared site navigation/footer.
- Define the selected colors, typography, shadows, and motion as reusable design tokens.
- Optimize generated images and reserve stable image dimensions to prevent layout shifts.
- Keep all forms presentation-only: validate the visible fields and show a clear demo confirmation without saving or sending information.
- Add unique search and social metadata for every page, using location-neutral wording until a real city is supplied.
- Do not connect external services, Google Business Profile, live messaging, or data storage in this prototype.

## Validation
- Check all navigation, menus, forms, contact actions, and chat interactions.
- Review the full experience at desktop and mobile sizes for overlaps, legibility, and conversion clarity.
- Confirm the homepage no longer contains the starter placeholder and each public page has distinct metadata.
